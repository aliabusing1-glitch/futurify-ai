Independent Research Initiative — Morocco

If workers learn to use AI, does their work actually improve?

AI literacy ≠ workplace capability

APTIVRA is a research pilot that tests this gap systematically — from AI training to measured workplace impact — through a repeatable field protocol beginning in Morocco.

The Frame

An academic framework, not a marketing pitch

AI adoption is accelerating faster than organizations can measure whether workers are using it effectively and responsibly. APTIVRA proposes to close this gap through a field research pilot — not through consulting tools or marketing claims.

Academic Partnership

Built on collaboration with researchers and academic institutions, not on selling consulting services.

Morocco to Africa

A field starting point in Morocco, with a potential expansion pathway toward North Africa and the Global South.

Independent Researcher

An initiative led by an independent researcher, with full transparency about its nature and current scope.

The Protocol

From baseline measurement to evidence

Five sequential stages that test the core hypothesis in the field, from starting point to publishable result.

01

Baseline

Measure current capability before any intervention.

02

Intervention

A training program focused on using AI tools in real work.

03

Workplace Application

Observe actual tool use within participants' daily tasks.

04

Post-Assessment

Re-measure to determine the actual change in performance.

05

Evidence

Documented results ready for academic review and publication.

Why Now?

AI adoption is accelerating faster than organizations can measure whether it is used effectively and responsibly — and the gap keeps widening quietly.

Why Morocco?

An emerging economy that represents a meaningful test case, on a pathway from Morocco to North Africa and the Global South.
